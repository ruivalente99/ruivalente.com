# RUIVALENTE.COM — The Soul Manifesto

## 1. Philosophical Foundations: Engineering Craftsmanship

ruivalente.com is the digital workshop, exhibition hall, and engineering home of Rui Valente. It is built to demonstrate what web engineering can be when stripped of corporate bloat, invasive trackers, and superficial embellishments.

### The Five Inviolable Virtues

1. **Understated Confidence & Editorial Dignity**
   - Consistent lowercase typographic discipline (`lowercase` class) across navigation, headings, and tags.
   - Clean, dark-mode first aesthetic with warm stone and subtle amber glows.
   - No bombastic self-aggrandizement; the quality of the systems speaks for itself.

2. **Performance & Lightweight Purity**
   - Zero layout shifts, instant navigation, and offline-friendly resilience.
   - Bundles compiled with Bun and optimized for high-density displays.
   - Respect for the user's bandwidth, battery, and cognitive attention.

3. **The Discipline of Simplicity (Ponytail Engineering)**
   - Powered by `@ruivalente99/bibliotheca` primitives (`Kbd`, `BentoGrid`, `Terminal`, `ProjectPreview`).
   - Semantic web standards before heavy runtime frameworks.
   - Clean, atomic commits with zero emojis.

4. **Playful Technical Depth**
   - Subtle easter eggs rewarding curiosity: the terminal emulator, interactive commit matrix, and the dark-side theme.
   - Interactive components designed with tactile physics and keyboard-first accessibility (`Cmd+K`).

5. **Transparency & Open Craft**
   - Every project showcased links directly to open source code, architectural documentation, and live demonstrations.
