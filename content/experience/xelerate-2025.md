## Overview

As a Frontend Engineer at Xelerate | Techem, I architect and build scalable, user-centric enterprise web applications and smart sub-metering energy management solutions, collaborating across distributed international engineering squads to deliver resilient, accessible, and high-performance interfaces.

Techem is a global market leader in smart sub-metering, digital device connectivity, and energy efficiency solutions. Within the Xelerate engineering division, our mission centers on transforming complex telemetry data streams into actionable, fluid, and intuitive digital interfaces for property managers, enterprise consumers, and internal operations.
